# Effects

<!--{"order": 60}-->

**Things that happen and are gone.** [Effects](/b3d-effect/) (volcanoes,
geysers, splashes, explosions, fireballs, sparks: particles, light and sound
on one clock, edited in the [effect tool](/effect-tool/)), [particles](/b3d-particles/) for smoke
and sparks, [trails](/b3d-trail/) behind anything that moves,
[sound](/b3d-sound/) placed in the world, and the [exploder](/b3d-exploder/)
for taking a model apart.
