# Characters and vehicles

<!--{"order": 70}-->

**Things you drive.** A [character](/b3d-biped/) who walks, runs, swims and
climbs; an [aircraft](/b3d-aircraft/) that hovers like a drone and flies like a
plane; a [car](/b3d-car/). All three take the same input, so you can get out of
one and into another (see [Input](/input/)). An [elevator](/b3d-elevator/) is a
platform you ride, and a [crowd](/b3d-crowd/) is a thousand figures for the
price of one.
