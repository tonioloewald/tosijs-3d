# Core

<!--{"order": 10}-->

**The scene itself, and the ways to put things in it.** [b3d](/tosi-b3d/) is the
one element everything else lives inside. After that: a
[box, sphere or ground](/b3d-primitives/) when you need something now, a
[model file](/b3d-loader/) when you have one, and a [library](/b3d-library/)
when you want many copies of the parts in one.
