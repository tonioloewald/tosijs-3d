## Fix before the tag

- [ ] **Screen-pinned (`screenAnchor`) frame panels are screen-placed inside a VR session, which overrides their VR anchor.** Verified. Lens: correctness. `src/frame-panel.ts:775`, `src/tosi-b3d.ts:4630`.
  - The XR path passes the spec on with `screenAnchor` still set. `update()` then calls `placeScreen()` every XR frame, and nothing stops it for a WebXR camera.
  - In a headset, the demo loadout (`demo/demo-utils.ts:563`, and the documented example in `inventory-box.ts:350`) is placed 1 m out, square-on, in the `eye` frame. Its position and size are worked out from the **flat** canvas size, instead of sitting on the left shoulder. CHANGELOG and commit c44b33bfd promise "shoulder in VR".
  - `focusPlace()` (line 490) also ignores `focusAnchor` in XR whenever `screenAnchor` is set.
  - Fix: keep screen pins flat-only. Either pass `{ ...spec, screenAnchor: undefined }` from the XR attach in tosi-b3d, or give `attachFramePanel` an explicit flat flag that controls both `placeScreen()` and the screen branch of `focusPlace()`.
  - **Clearing check:** run `bun run xr /tosi-b3d/` (and the Biped Playground) and confirm the loadout sits on the left shoulder in both eyes. Also add a unit test that fails with the fix reverted, for example "an XR attach with screenAnchor uses the preset anchor".
  - The emulated headset covers this, so it does not need a real device.

## Verify or fix (unconfirmed major)

- [ ] **(unverified) On a page, the two-tap carry is cancelled by the same pointerup that picks the item up.** Lens: correctness. `src/carry-box.ts:92`, `src/inventory-box.ts:106, 169, 284`.
  - `box` activates on pointerup, so `carry.pickUp(…,'tap')` runs inside that event. The documented `window.addEventListener('pointerup', () => carry.cancel())` then fires and clears the carry.
  - The lens reproduced this in happy-dom.
  - Simply removing the listener leaves a drag released outside every panel stuck in the held state.
  - Fix: handle miss-cancellation inside the library (`carryOverlay`), after the panels have handled the release, and only when `carry.state.mode === 'drag'`. Remove the cancel lines from the doc examples, or change them to check for drag mode.
  - Clearing check: a happy-dom test that taps a tile, sends a window pointerup, and finds the item still held.

- [ ] (unverified, nit) **Two sticky-row display problems in `box`.** `src/box.ts:682`.
  - A focused sticky child draws its focus ring at its scrolled `content` position instead of in the unscrolled `pinned` group.
  - `pinnedBg` falls back to `background ?? 'none'`, so with no box background, rows scrolling under a sticky strip show through it, even though hit-testing treats them as hidden.
  - Fix: draw the ring in `pinned` (or offset it by the scroll), and either require a background for sticky rows or document that the strip is transparent.
