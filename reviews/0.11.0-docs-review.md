# Docs review: tosijs-3d 0.11.0

Asked for by Tonio with the 0.11.0 release: "Our docs are getting pretty huge."
The pre-tag gate's docs lens only looked at what changed since 0.10.7; this looks
at the whole site.

**How it was done.** Four read-only passes ran in parallel, each over one slice of
the 249 pages: the changelog, migration and landing pages; the 30 largest pages;
and the other ~190, split in two. Each measured where a page's words go, found
the first demo against the first table, grepped for history markers ("used to",
"Tonio", issue numbers), and diffed attribute tables against `static
initAttributes`. Claims marked ✅ were re-checked against the code afterwards.
The rest are the reviewers' readings and should be confirmed when fixed.

**Size today.** 249 pages, about 237,000 words. The CHANGELOG is 66,000 of
them. The median page is 420 words. About 20 of the 30 largest pages are big
because of their demo code, which is fine; the prose bloat sits in a few pages.

---

## 1. Bugs the review found (not doc problems)

- ✅ **The glass gamepad drew nothing on an adopter's site. Fixed in 69a6f28a7**
  (for 0.11.1). Its default art was fetched from `/gamepad-*.svg`; only our
  doc site serves those, and they are not in the package. The art is now built
  in.
- ✅ **Two more defaults fetch from our `static/` folder. Not fixed yet; both are
  opt-in.**
  - `xr-reticle="on"` loads `/reticle.svg` (`tosi-b3d.ts`, around line 1989).
  - `b3d-crowd`'s baseline `skinnedUrl` defaults to `/omnidude.glb`.
  - Embed the reticle as the gamepad now is. Point the crowd at the CDN
    (`assetUrl`).

## 2. Decisions for Tonio

1. **Archive the old changelog.** Words per minor: 0.5 1.5k, 0.6 1.8k, 0.7
   21.1k, 0.8 18.5k, 0.9 0.9k, 0.10 20.6k, 0.11 1.5k.
   - Move 0.5–0.9 to a `CHANGELOG-archive.md`, shipped in the package but not in
     the site's nav. Saves about 44k words.
   - Folding the 0.10.x patch notes in too saves about 20k more. 0.11.0's
     opening paragraph already says it collects them.
   - Migration.md: keep 0.9→0.10 and 0.10→0.11 and archive the rest. Saves
     about 2.2k words.
2. **A rule for changelog entries: say what changed and what to do; the story
   goes in the commit or the card.** The median bullet is 57 words, but 51 run
   over 150 and the worst is 662 ("The biped swims", 0.7.3). Even 0.11.0's
   shadows entry narrates its reproduction. An 80-word cap on what we keep saves
   about 3.7k words.
3. **A rule for doc pages: state the current behaviour and its reason, never
   who reported it or what it used to do.** Tonio quotes, "used to", issue and
   board numbers, and "per CLAUDE.md" (a file adopters cannot see) are spread
   across about 40 pages. This is the biggest single source of prose bloat.
4. **Merge helper pages into the page that uses them.** Roughly 60 pages are a
   pure helper's design notes rather than something a reader calls; merging
   them takes them out of the nav. Proposed pairs (from two reviewers):
   - water-normal, caustics, water-shore → b3d-water
   - cloud-field → b3d-cloud-deck
   - ambient-budget, ambient-leaves → b3d-ambient
   - manipulator, manipulator-view → b3d-manipulator
   - table-layout → table; key-layout, text-edit → keyboard
   - widgets3d-layout → widgets3d; icon-name → svg-icons; embed-font →
     svg-texture
   - panel-layer, rounded-rect, dialog-placement, panel-orbit, popup-chrome →
     frame-panel / popup-surface
   - light-settings, light-editor → b3d-lamp
   - warhead, destroyable, resource → the combat elements; radar → b3d-radar;
     hud, hud-math → b3d-hud
   - swim-aim, mantle, camera-fit → a "movement model" section on b3d-biped
   - atmosphere → b3d-fog; terrain-grid → b3d-terrain; touch-gesture →
     touch-orbit
   - world-topology → world-store; noise-volume → noise-texture
   - galaxy-data, star-populations → b3d-galaxy
   - hardware-gamepad, gamepad-svg, keyboard-gamepad, xr-gamepad →
     virtual-gamepad
   - lightning → b3d-lightning; light-rays → b3d-light-shafts
   - bone-mask → animation-layers; arc → angle-field; curve → curve-field
   - gas-giant-material → gas-giant; sol, system-layout → b3d-star-system
   - climate-noise → b3d-decorator; wreck-fall → b3d-death
   - library-mesh → b3d-prop; surroundings-probe → surroundings; text-entry →
     keyboard-gamepad; handler-of → one paragraph on `ui`

   This is the right shape, I think, but it is a big structural change. It
   needs the doc system's `parent` and toc kept in step, and the page URLs that
   go away need a decision (redirect, or accept the 404).
5. **One source of truth for the nav.** Every toc entry also declares the same
   `parent` in its source, so the two will drift. Either generate the tocs
   (getting-started.md already uses `<!-- toc --><!-- /toc -->`) or drop the
   `parent`s.

## 3. Design essays to move out of reference pages

- **b3d-crowd (6,942 words).** About 4,500 are benchmarking history and
  miniatures-game design: "MEASURED ON A QUEST 3", "Would a big battle fit in a
  headset?", "A stadium…".
  - Move them to DECISIONS.md or MINIATURES-DESIGN.md.
  - Keep the demo, how to read it, the rig bake, Attributes, and a three-line
    summary of the Quest ceilings (about 750 crowd figures in VR, about 20
    skinned rigs).
- **world-contract.** "The driver belongs OFF-THREAD" and "Testing a driver:
  THREE parts" (about 1,000 words) are about method, not the types. Move them
  to ARIOSTO-SIM.md.
- **starfield-codec (785 words).** Internal encoding design; move it to
  GALAXY-DESIGN.md.
- **light-modulation.** Cut it to the contract and link curve-program's demo.
  Saves about 500 words.

## 4. Factual fixes (stale or wrong)

- ✅ **b3d-aircraft.** The table says `missileSpeed` is `55`; the code says `90`.
  About 20 attributes are not in the table.
- ✅ **b3d-panel.** It has both `flatFrame` and `flat-frame` rows, worded
  differently. The JS API and the events (`show`/`hide`/`toggle`/`attention`,
  `paneltoggle`/`systempanels`) sit under "## Attributes" and need their own
  heading.
- ✅ **b3d-controllable.** The example uses `this.meshNode`, which only
  b3d-aircraft has. Copied, it does nothing.
- ✅ **svg-texture.** It says a theme font reaches an in-scene panel only if the
  device has it installed. `registerSvgFont` exists to fix exactly that; link
  it.
- ✅ **formations.** The demo passes `orbitCam` the deprecated radian
  `alpha`/`beta`. Use `alphaDeg`/`betaDeg`.
- **tosi-b3d.** The core page documents 12 of its 36 attributes. Missing:
  `quality`, the `ssao*` set, `pauseWhenHidden`, `startPaused`, `timeScale`, the
  wind set, `stats`, `pixelRatio`, `xrGrid`, `xrReticle`, `scenePanelOpen`,
  `enterXrOnResume` and others. This is the most-read page.
- **b3d-biped.** It documents 15 of its 33 attributes (`jumpSpeed`,
  `sneakSpeed`, `wadeDepth`, `eyeHeight`, `climbReach`, …).
- **b3d-water.** Missing: `waveLength`, `normalMap`, `wind`,
  `windDirectionX/Y`, `underwaterFog`, `underwaterMurk`, `fogTransition`.
- **Smaller gaps:**
  - planet: `biome`, `seaShallow`, `seaDeep`, `rings`, …
  - skybox: `mie*`, `skyboxSize`, …
  - lamp: `dirX/Y/Z`, area `width`/`height`
  - svg-plane: `castShadow`, `receiveShadows`, `placement`
  - clouds: `wind*`
  - controller: `player`
  - turret and launcher: `meshName`, `library`, …
  - physics: `wasmUrl`
  - radar: `acquireRange`
  - elevator and primitives: `meshName`
  - glass-gamepad: `scale`, `deadzone`, `maxZone`
- **b3d-cloud-deck.** "First pass — the LOOK is not tuned" lists problems its
  own items mark fixed. Delete the section.
- **game-controller.** It is legacy, but the page presents it as the way to
  wire input. Mark it deprecated and point to b3d-controller.
- **Placeholder "Example" blocks** (an import and a comment): ambient-budget,
  perf-probe, xr-gamepad, keyboard-gamepad, hud-math, destroyable-behavior.
- **flow-layout.** One paragraph is in the wrong place, so a bullet list is cut
  off from its lead-in.
- **b3d-radar-blip** ends with a stray `#`.
- **Two link styles.** Some pages use `[x](?x.ts)` and some `[[x]]`; pick one.

## 7. Navigation

- No page is unreachable, and no toc link is dead.
- **reference.md** mixes Changelog/Migration with the noise and random pages;
  give those their own group.
- **Pure models listed at the top level** contradict getting-started's "tucked
  under an element": portal-transform, spatial-transform, asset-url,
  demo-utils, sphere-map, planet-map, space-backdrop.
- **water.md's toc** has one entry; its children are reachable only through
  b3d-water.
- **README.md** is missing weather and lightning, combat, inventory, lamps,
  physics, and the system bar and panels. One line linking the twelve landing
  pages would cover them.
- **llms.txt** never mentions `/attributes.txt`, which is the index agents are
  meant to use. It lists pages alphabetically, so Getting started is at line
  264 of 330, and many of its summaries are sentence fragments (the line after
  a bold lead). It is generated in tosijs-ui, so the fix is upstream.

## Suggested order

1. The factual fixes (§4) and the two remaining static-path bugs (§1), as
   0.11.1 with the gamepad fix. These are small and correct whatever is decided
   about the rest.
2. The changelog and migration archive (§2.1). It is one change and removes
   about 65,000 words, more than everything else together.
3. The two rules (§2.2–3), then the design-essay moves (§3) and the reorders
   (§5).
4. The merges (§2.4) and the nav source of truth (§2.5): a structural pass of
   their own, once the URL question is answered.
