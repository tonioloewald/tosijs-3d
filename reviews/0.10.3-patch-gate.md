## Fix before the tag (verified majors)

### 1. The terrain seen from above is the mirror image of the globe built from it (`src/sphere-map.ts:109`; correctness, verified, severity uncertain)

- **What is wrong:** `groundToUv` sets `v = worldV + z/(pi*R)` and `v` runs from the north pole down, so +z is south. The doc table says ground is "metres east and north".
- **Measured:** `groundToLatLon(100,200).lat` = 42.1352 and `groundToLatLon(100,201).lat` = 42.1209, so latitude falls as z rises.
- **Mirror:** the ground-to-globe map reverses orientation (ground cross product is -1 against up, globe is +1 against outward). No compensating flip exists in `terrain.planetSurface()` or `b3d-planet.ts:563`.
- **Effect:** a pin's lat/lon is right, but the shapes around it are mirrored between the top-down terrain and the globe. An adopter who adds +z to go "north" goes south.
- **Fix:** the terrain's own wrap is fixed, so mirror the direction in sphere-map/`planetSurface` for the terrain path. Correct the "east and north" wording to match. Say in `latLonAt`'s docstring that x,z are logical coordinates (scene position plus `originOffset`).
- **Check that clears it:**
  - A test in `sphere-map.test.ts` asserting the sign of d(lat)/dz, which fails with the fix reverted.
  - A test asserting ground-to-globe preserves orientation, which also fails with the fix reverted.
  - Drive `/sphere-map/` and confirm a feature east of the pin on the terrain is east of it on the globe.

### 2. Planet weather is a second cloud, storm and lightning implementation, and the divergence from the agreed plan is unrecorded (`src/planet-clouds.ts:78`; blast-radius, verified, severity uncertain)

- **What shipped:** its own GLSL field, its own storm model (`planet.storms`, `PlanetStorm`) and its own lightning schedule (shader hash slots on `performance.now()`). It imports nothing from `cloud-field.ts`, `weather.ts` or `lightning.ts`.
- **What was agreed:** card #3232 says the cloud layer is drawn from the deck's field, and storms come from "the SAME model the ground uses". The card's last comment still lists that as to do.
- **Effect:** a hurricane pinned at a lat/lon on the globe has no counterpart in `weatherAt` or the deck on the ground. The same release's CHANGELOG says "the planet you see from orbit is then the ground you land on".
- **Mitigation:** the deck's 2D tiled field cannot wrap a sphere without a pole pinch, so a separate field is partly justified. The storm and lightning divergence is the solid part.
- **Fix (no code needed to ship):**
  - Record on #3232 (or in `DECISIONS.md`) that what shipped is an orbit-only approximation, and why.
  - Say on the `clouds`/`storms` attribute rows of `b3d-planet` that ground weather does not agree yet and that `planet.storms` is provisional.
  - File the card for step 3 (storms as weather cells projected through sphere-map).
- **Check that clears it:** the note exists on #3232, the attribute rows carry the provisional wording, and the follow-up card exists. Which way it goes long-term is your call; the record can be made now either way.

This diff makes `rootNode` the public way to place a planet. The shipped demo escapes only because its globe leaves `biome` off.
