Demos

Whole scenes you can walk around in, rather than one component at a time.

Everything here is assembled from the same elements documented in the other sections — the point of a demo is that the pieces have to agree with each other, which is where the interesting bugs live. Several of the fixes recorded across this site were found by someone playing one of these for five minutes, not by reading the code.
