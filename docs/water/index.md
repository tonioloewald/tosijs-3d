# Water

<!--{"order": 50}-->

**A sea that knows its shore.** [b3d-water](/b3d-water/) is the surface:
waves, reflections, and with `shore="on"` the surf, shallows and sea ice that
come from knowing where the land is. The shore and ice rules, the caustics
on the seabed and what it means to be under water are all on that page.
